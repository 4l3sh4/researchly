Reviewer Guidelines

Review proposals fairly and consistently. Your feedback directly affects funding decisions.

Evaluation Criteria ▸
  • Relevance: Fits the grant scheme objectives and department scope.
  • Novelty & contribution: What’s new here? Why should anyone fund it?
  • Methodology quality: Clear approach, measurable outcomes, realistic methods.
  • Feasibility: Timeline, resources, team capability, and risks are credible.
  • Budget realism: Costs match the work; no inflated or missing items.
  • Impact: Benefits to community/industry/academia; clear stakeholders.
  • Clarity: Well-structured writing, complete sections, no vague filler.
Evaluation Approach ▸

Be consistent. Don’t reward “nice writing” if the plan is weak. Don’t punish simple writing if the plan is solid.

  • Read the abstract first, then check if the methodology actually delivers it.
  • Identify assumptions and failure points (data access, permissions, equipment, skills).
  • Look for measurable deliverables (models, prototypes, datasets, papers, deployment).
  • Flag missing details: model choice, evaluation metrics, baseline comparisons, validation plans.
  • Prefer specific, actionable feedback over general complaints.

Numerical scoring isn’t required. Your written feedback must justify your recommendation.

Recommendation Guidelines ▸
  • Recommended: Strong proposal; minor issues only.
  • Revision required: Fixable, but missing key details (methods, evaluation, budget, feasibility).
  • Rejected: Fundamentally weak, unrealistic, unethical, or not aligned with the scheme.

If you choose Revision required, state exactly what must be changed and how it will be verified.

Ethical Review Conduct ▸
  • Confidentiality: Do not share proposal content outside the platform.
  • Conflict of interest: If you have one, decline the assignment.
  • Fairness: Judge the proposal, not the person or department.
  • Professionalism: Be direct, but not insulting. Point to evidence.
  • Integrity: No plagiarism checks? Then don’t accuse—flag as “needs verification”.
